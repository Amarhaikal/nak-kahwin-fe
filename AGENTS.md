## 💍 Business & Application Context: Nak Kahwin
**Nak Kahwin** (*"Want to Marry"* in Malay) is a collaborative wedding planning and tracking application designed for couples to plan their wedding details together.

### Core Business Pillars
1. **Shared Planning (Groom & Bride Collaboration)**:
   * The app supports dual roles of **Groom** and **Bride**.
   * Collaborative updates on shared assets (budget, savings, checklists).

2. **Event Scheduling**:
   * Tracks preparation details, venue, dates, and times for key traditional milestones:
     * **Nikah** (solemnization ceremony)
     * **Tunang** (engagement ceremony)

3. **Financial Coordination**:
   * **Budgeting**: Cataloging and tracking vendor expenses and payments.
   * **Savings**: Goal-oriented savings tracking to ensure the couple reaches their target wedding budget.

4. **Task Checklist**:
   * Timed checklist to ensure pre-wedding preparations are completed on schedule.
